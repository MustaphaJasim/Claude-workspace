# Westmont Global – SMS Do-Not-Contact Policy

Version 1, 2026-10-09. This is the written policy required for telemarketing under
47 CFR 64.1200(d)(1). Available on request.

1. **Who it covers:** all SMS sent by Westmont Global Ltd from its Ringover US number
   (+1 945-363-7786) to clients, prospective clients and candidates.
2. **Opt-out requests:** a reply of STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, or any message
   that reasonably means "don't text me", is an opt-out. It is recorded on Westmont's
   internal do-not-text list (SMS ledger + Google Sheet "OptOuts" tab), and in
   Ringover where possible, as soon as it is seen. Westmont's target is the same
   business day; the legal maximum is 10 business days. No further marketing texts are
   sent to that number. One confirmation text is allowed.
3. **Retention:** opt-outs are kept for at least 5 years and are never removed
   automatically. Only Mustapha can remove one, in the Ringover app, and only on the
   person's own written request.
4. **Identification:** every marketing text names the individual sender (Mustapha) and
   Westmont Global, and gives the opt-out instruction. The sending number takes calls and texts.
5. **National Do-Not-Call Registry:** numbers are checked against the Registry (scrub no
   more than 31 days old) before any marketing text, unless there is an established
   business relationship or written permission.
6. **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 9am–7pm in the recipient's local time.
7. **Training:** anyone sending SMS for Westmont (currently Mustapha, assisted by Claude
   through the Westmont SMS Outreach system) follows this policy. The system enforces
   points 2, 4, 5 and 6 automatically.
